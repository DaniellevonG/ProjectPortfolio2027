Project Overview

ProjectCall Centre Training
Project Run DateAug 2025 – Oct 2025
RoleLead Designer

What is the project about?

This project focuses on the development of a specialised AI-powered training platform designed to accelerate the onboarding process for new call centre employees.

At its core, the initiative aims to showcase the tangible value of AI in solving real-world business problems, specifically bridging the gap between theoretical training and practical application. By moving away from static learning materials, the platform creates an immersive environment where agents can practice in a safe, low-risk setting. The goal is to produce a tool that not only educates but also builds confidence through realistic simulations, ultimately resulting in higher-quality customer service interactions.

Brief

This platform addresses the growing industry need for realistic customer call simulations. It combines interactive assessments with advanced AI features to create a comprehensive “dynamic learning” ecosystem. The system allows trainees to engage with simulated customers—either through a voice-enabled model or simplified interaction flows—to practice handling various scenarios before taking live calls.

Key functional components

Customer Call Simulations: The platform features realistic scenarios where the AI acts as the customer. This prepares agents for the unpredictability of live calls.
Smart Feedback Loop: The system includes integrated quizzes and conversational interactions. Crucially, if an employee provides an incorrect answer or handles a situation poorly, the system provides conversational feedback explaining why the response was incorrect and how to improve.
Real-Time Tech Features:
  • Live Transcription: Converts speech to text instantly for review.
  • Sentiment Analysis: Monitors the “customer’s” emotional state during the call, teaching agents to read emotional cues.
Dynamic Learning Modes: The platform adapts to the user, offering different modes of difficulty or scenario types to ensure continuous improvement.

Discovery

Chosen Design methodWaterfall
Time For Ideation2 Working Days

Step 1: Establish Main Linear Flow

We received 5 documents from the client outlining the different sections and scenarios needed for the platform. Us as a team first outlined the main linear success journey to use as reference to start wireframes. During our project onboarding we were told to create an easy process and to focus in on the main functionality.

We worked through the documents and discovered that they’re filled with example question-and-answer sheets used for training. The documents will be used to inform the copy. Due to time constraints to deliver wireframes we’ll be including more finalised copy to give the client a more realistic view of what to expect, more traditional wireframes don’t allow for this.

Step 2: High fidelity Wireframes

Each one of the designers took responsibility for pieces of the flow we’re most excited about/already have some ideas for, after collectively brainstorming and establishing style parameters. We split off and put together a complete linear flow.
