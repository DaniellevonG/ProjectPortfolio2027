What is the project about?

This platform is used to onboard individual clients and entities for overall money management services (FICA and FACTA compliance, transactions, investments, property guarantees, FOREX inwards and outwards).

Brief

I was tasked to redesign and simplify the existing Corporate Saver Servicing platform to improve the servicing onboarding process for both Nedbank agents and clients. As well as completely redesign the system structure and user interface of multiple features.

Current System – Upfront Evaluation

The last time this system was touched by a designer, was in 2005. Around x amount of people work on this system every single day, earning the bank about R x 000 000 per financial year. It is extremely important from the beginning to uphold the standard that is set by the current design team and better everything from all perspectives (talk about a deep dive).

Before starting the redesign, we needed to understand the current systems strengths and weaknesses. We conducted a thorough evaluation, gathering feedback from users and stakeholders to identify pain points and areas for improvement. Key issues included:

Poor Navigation

Not because of the initial view, But because of...

Outdated UI

In the words of our product owner, “it looks like an Excel spreadsheet.”

Confusing Information Structure

The structure of information is grouped incorrectly and there is unnecessary information that can be derived. Hover
here

Visual and Cognitive Overload

The form is appears cluttered, has complicated menus, and shows system inconsistency when looking at the other screens.

Current System – “Source of Truth” Diagram

My design senior and I received access to the system and started going through every single flow. We took screenshots of every screen, in every state, and mapped out every flow in FigJam. Yes, it was crazy. Yes, it was insane, But it gave us the ability to see exactly what has to be changed, what can be introduced, and what has become obsolete.
